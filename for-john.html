For John

Dizzy and still on top

Two years of a world that will not hold still, and John is still the one holding the crown.

He shows up for everything. The games, the group chat, the long arguments about who deserves what. Most people would have turned this into an excuse by now. John turned it into material.

This page has nothing to do with football. It is just here to say the obvious out loud. You are appreciated. You are loved. Your family thinks you are tremendous company on the steady days and the tilting ones alike.

Keep the crown as long as you like. Honestly, nobody is trying very hard to take it.
