For John

Two years of the room moving

Two years is a long time to live with a room that will not sit still.

John has spent them showing up anyway — to the games, to the group chat, to the arguments about who deserves the crown. Dizziness is invisible to everyone except the person carrying it, and he has carried this without once asking anyone else to carry it with him.

So here is a page with nothing to do with football. You are appreciated. You are loved. Your family sees all of it — the good days, the ones where the floor tilts, and every single time you turned up regardless.

Keep the crown as long as you like. Nobody here is really competing with you.
